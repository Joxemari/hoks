    [01] — code

    code proposes.

    [02] — geometry

    geometry translates.

    a seed decides. the same rule, executed once, takes a form — and it would take that same form a thousand times.

    the block, before the dots
    [03] — matter

    matter negotiates.

    printed at 300 dpi, the work meets paper, pigment and grain. the grain belongs to the seed too.
