square · horizontal (DIN) · Joxemari Gallastegi

Two bodies meet and weld. There is no over and no under, no incision: one single mass. And the ground, which in the crossing passed between, is here trapped — it survives only where the mass closes around it. It stops being a line and becomes an island with an edge. That is an eye, and the eyes are the work: the void is not what is left over, it is what the mass is there to define.
